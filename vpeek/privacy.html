Privacy

VPeek stores copies and cuts you make on this PC only, in a local folder under your user profile. Clipboard contents are not uploaded, sold, or sent to us.

To implement paste chords, the app installs a keyboard hook. It does not keep a log of what you type. It looks at keys only while Ctrl is held, and it never swallows Ctrl+C or Ctrl+X.

Windows belonging to known password managers are skipped so those copies are not saved in the ledger.

Buying the app is handled by the Microsoft Store and your Microsoft account. We do not receive your card number. We do not run our own analytics.

Questions: use the support email on the Microsoft Store listing for VPeek.
