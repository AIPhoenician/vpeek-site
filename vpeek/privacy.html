Privacy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

This policy is for VPeek, a product of ForumCore (Petr Jaroch, IČO 03330389).

VPeek keeps copies and cuts on your PC only, in a folder under your Windows user profile. We do not receive clipboard contents, keystrokes, or analytics. There is no VPeek account and no VPeek server for this product.

A keyboard hook is used so paste chords work. It does not log typing. It looks at keys only while Ctrl is held. Ctrl+C and Ctrl+X are never swallowed. Known password-manager windows are skipped so those copies are not stored in the ledger.

Purchase and refunds are handled by the Microsoft Store and your Microsoft account. We do not see your card number.
