2. The Product

BetterCtrlV is a Windows utility that keeps a local ledger of what you copy and cut, and lets you paste from that ledger with keyboard chords. Copy and cut share one ledger. Cut also removes the original in the app you cut from, as Windows already does.

A short hold of Ctrl+V shows recent items. Numbers 1–9 select an older copy. S and D move through the list. K removes the highlighted line. Holding L clears the list and the live clipboard. Letting go of Ctrl pastes the highlighted item. A normal tap of Ctrl+V still pastes the newest clipboard item.

The ledger is stored only on your PC. BetterCtrlV does not upload clipboard contents. It uses a keyboard hook so the chords work. It does not record ordinary typing. It cannot see keys in elevated windows. Some games and exclusive-input apps may ignore the hook. Do not use BetterCtrlV where a keyboard hook is forbidden.

We may change, suspend, or discontinue features when needed for operations, security, or product direction. We do not guarantee that BetterCtrlV will work in every Windows app or after every Windows update.
