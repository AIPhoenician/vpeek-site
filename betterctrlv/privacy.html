Privacy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

This policy is for BetterCtrlV, a product of ForumCore (Petr Jaroch, IČO 03330389).

BetterCtrlV keeps copies and cuts on your PC only, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\BetterCtrlV under your Windows user profile. We do not receive clipboard contents, keystrokes, or analytics. There is no BetterCtrlV account and no BetterCtrlV server for this product. The packaged app does not declare an internet capability (internetClient).

The ledger file is encrypted with Windows DPAPI for your Windows user. If that file cannot be decrypted, BetterCtrlV starts with an empty list rather than reading it as plaintext. Text longer than about 1 MB is not added to the ledger (the live clipboard still works). Uninstall does not automatically delete this folder.

A keyboard hook is used so paste chords work. It does not log typing. It looks at keys only while Ctrl is held, and only when Ctrl is the sole modifier. Ctrl+C, Ctrl+X, Ctrl+Shift+V, and AltGr are never swallowed. Known password-manager windows are skipped. Clipboard formats that ask not to be included in clipboard history are honoured, so those copies are not stored in the ledger.

To clear one line, hold Ctrl+V and press K. To clear the list and the live clipboard, hold L. The tray menu also has Clear history and Open data folder (the folder contains the encrypted ledger, settings, and a small diagnostic log).

This website is static. It does not load scripts from other sites. The typeface is stored with the site. A first-party cookie named forumcore_locale stores your language choice for one year.

Purchase and refunds are handled by the Microsoft Store and your Microsoft account. We do not see your card number.
